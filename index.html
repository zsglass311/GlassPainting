About Glass Painting

Four Decades of Work, Built on Referrals

Glass Painting is a family-owned, licensed and insured house painting company based in Braselton, Georgia. For more than 40 years, nearly every job we've done has come from a recommendation: a past customer telling a friend, a neighbor, or a family member about us.

We've earned that by doing careful work, being straightforward about what a job needs, and treating every home as if it were our own. Whether someone sent you our way or you found us here, you can expect the same.
