Our story

Four decades of referrals. Now you can find us online, too.

Glass Painting is a family business, and for more than 40 years we've grown the old-fashioned way: one home at a time. We never needed to advertise. Our customers recommended us to their friends, family, and neighbors, and those neighbors called us.

A reputation like that comes from careful work, honest advice about what a job really needs, and treating every house like it's our own. Whether a neighbor sent you or you found us here, that's what you can expect.

  • Respect for your home

    Furniture, floors, and landscaping protected, and work areas cleaned up every day.

  • Prep done right

    A great finish starts underneath: cleaning, patching, sanding, caulking, and priming.

  • Straight answers

    A clear quote up front and honest advice about what your home needs, and what it doesn't.

  • Truly local

    We live and work here. Our name and our reputation go on every job we do.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the quote really free?

Yes. We'll come look at your project and give you a quote at no cost and with no obligation.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Glass Painting is licensed and insured, so you and your home are protected while we work.

Do you paint commercial buildings or apartments?

No. We work exclusively on single-family homes, inside and out. Focusing on houses is how we've gotten so good at them.

Do I need to pick colors before I contact you?

Not at all. We're happy to talk through colors and finishes with you when we visit.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Braselton, Georgia and work throughout the surrounding area, including Hoschton, Jefferson, Buford, Flowery Branch, Gainesville, and more. If you're nearby, just ask.
